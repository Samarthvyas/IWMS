About IWMS

Intelligent Waste Management System

What is IWMS?

The Intelligent Waste Management System (IWMS) is a smart platform designed to simplify waste reporting and management. Citizens can submit waste reports with images and location information, while administrators can monitor, classify and manage those reports.

IWMS uses Artificial Intelligence to help identify the type of waste from an uploaded image and automatically assign an appropriate priority to the report.

📷
Easy Reporting

Citizens can quickly report waste by submitting an image and location.

🤖
AI Classification

Artificial Intelligence analyzes uploaded waste images and predicts the waste category.

📊
Smart Management

Administrators can monitor reports, manage priorities and update report status.
